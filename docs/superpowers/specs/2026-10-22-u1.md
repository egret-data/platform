## Decisions taken here

- **Which pages are narrow.** Forms and form editors (alert and report editor, create and register pages, choose a visualization) and the
  Workspace settings pane. Workspace settings keeps its section nav at the list's left edge and caps the pane beside it at 960 px, so the
  nav and the title do not move sideways and the pane is the column.
- **Tools stay full width.** SQL Lab, the chart editor, a dashboard, the flow editor, an app's viewer and editor and an endpoint's detail
  are work surfaces: full width, compact 16 px title. SQL Lab gets a visible compact title in its tab bar (it had only an `sr-only` one).
  The chart editor's title is its name field, already 16 px; its `sr-only` `h1` stays because a heading cannot be an input.
- **One left edge.** `PageContainer` keeps the Explore list padding (`p-4` inside `main`'s `p-6`), so lists do not move and Control Plane
  pages move 16 px right to meet them.
- **Hand-rolled tab strips that stay.** `DashboardTabBar`, `PanelTabStrip` and `NamespaceFiles` are document tabs (renamed, closed,
  dragged), not sections or panels, and keep their own strip; the rulebook lists them. The Data API statement switcher uses
  `tabsTriggerClass("underline")` but stays a plain `role="tab"` element, because the trashed endpoint's `fieldset disabled` must not
  disable switching.
- **Status versus type.** `StatusBadge` is for a state (active, archived, allow, deny, success, paused). A type or a count (`s3`, `trino`,
  `built-in`, `3`) stays a plain `Badge`.
