- S0b T3 (pool hand-off): the brief's design — the pool accepts the shim's connection and passes it to a worker with `socket.send_fds` — lost sessions on macOS under marimo: the worker's first `recv_fds` on the passed socket returned **0 bytes, 0 fds** and the shim got EOF (`shim: bad reply []`, marimo "Kernel exited during startup (exit code 71)"). Seen 1 of 5 viewers (first `s0b_startup.py pool` run, which then timed out at 300 s: no `s0b-pool.json` from that design), 1 of 5, 1 of 5, and 4 of 12 (with every fork serialised against the hand-off, so not a fork race). A standalone stress of 20 clients without marimo did not reproduce it. Cause not proven; passing a Unix socket that is itself about to carry SCM_RIGHTS is the suspect. Replaced by: every worker inherits the pool's listening socket at spawn, and the pool "arms" one warm worker at a time to `accept()` once (`kernel_pool.py` docstring); no socket is ever in flight. After that: 0 failures in 12 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 3 + 2 + 2 marimo sessions. Consequence: the pool holds size+1 warm workers (N idle + 1 armed).
- S0b T3 (hard requirements): (1) kill-on-disconnect — a claimed worker runs a thread blocked on `conn.recv`; on EOF it `killpg`s its own group (only if it leads one, i.e. after marimo's `os.setsid()`, `marimo/_runtime/runtime.py:2505`) and `os._exit`s. `tests/test_kernel_pool.py` SIGKILLs the shim client and the worker pid is gone within 5 s; with the thread disabled the same test fails (`worker … outlived its client`). The pool reaps claimed workers (`proc.wait`), else a dead worker stays a zombie and `kill(pid, 0)` still succeeds. (2) cwd — marimo's `Popen` for the kernel passes no `cwd` (`marimo/_session/managers/ipc.py` start_kernel), so the kernel inherits the server's cwd; the shim now sends `__S0_SHIM_CWD__` in the env payload and the worker pops it and `chdir`s (asserted in the same test with `lsof -d cwd`). Env read only at interpreter start-up (`PYTHONPATH`, `PYTHONHASHSEED`, anything marimo reads at import) is the pool's, not the launcher's. (3) `S0_REAL_PYTHON`/`S0_KERNEL_SOCKET` survive: `construct_kernel_env` copies `os.environ` and pops only `MARIMO_SANDBOX_MODE`, `MARIMO_SANDBOX_BACKEND`, `MARIMO_MANAGE_SCRIPT_METADATA` (`marimo/_session/managers/ipc.py:215-265`); confirmed live (kernels launched via the shim).
